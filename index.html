Smash Counterpick Helper

Pick your main and 1 or 2 secondaries. For every fighter in Ultimate, see which of yours to pick.

Picks are estimates built from UltRank's May 2026 tier list, a few broad traits per fighter (speed, weight, range, projectiles, recovery, kill power), and a bonus for your main. There's no current, complete pro matchup chart to pull from, so tap "Real results" on any row to see actual tournament win rates for that matchup on smashmatchup.com.
